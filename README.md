# 🚀 Gen AI Prompt Constructor

Welcome to the **Gen AI Prompt Constructor**!

Think of Artificial Intelligence (AI) like a super-smart robot that knows almost everything, but sometimes gets confused if you don't give it exact instructions. This app is your **Command Center**. It helps you build the _perfect_ instructions (called "prompts") so the AI does exactly what you want, every single time.

Whether you are writing an email, coding a video game, making cool AI art, or doing deep research, this tool has a special tab to help you build the ultimate prompt.

---

## 🛠️ How to Get Started

1. **Open the App:** Just open `index.html` in your web browser.
2. **Pick a Tab:** Look at the top menu and click the tab that matches what you want to do (like writing, coding, or drawing).
3. **Fill in the Blanks:** Answer the simple questions in the boxes.
4. **Watch the Magic:** Look at the **Live Preview** on the right side. The app automatically stitches your answers together into a super-prompt!
5. **Copy & Paste:** Click the "Copy" button and paste your prompt into your favorite AI (like Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude).

## 🗂️ The 8 Magic Tabs (With Examples!)

### 1. Workspace PTCF (The All-Rounder)

This is your go-to tab for everyday tasks like writing emails, essays, or summaries. It uses the **PTCF** rule:

- **Persona:** Who is the AI pretending to be?
- **Task:** What do you want it to do?
- **Context:** What background info does it need?
- **Format:** How should it look?

> **Example:**
>
> - **Persona:** A friendly science teacher.
> - **Task:** Explain how black holes work.
> - **Context:** The audience is a class of 6th graders.
> - **Format:** Use bullet points and include one fun fact at the end.

### 2. DESIGN (Image Generation)

Want to create amazing AI pictures? Don't just say "draw a dog." Tell the AI exactly what the camera sees!

- **Description:** A happy dog wearing sunglasses.
- **Environment:** On a sunny beach in Australia.
- **Style:** 3D Pixar movie style.
- **Illumination:** Bright morning sunlight.
- **Gradation (Quality):** 8k resolution, super detailed.
- **Nuances:** Negative: no humans, no blurry background.

### 3. Agent Mode (The Problem Solver)

Use this when you have a big project (like fixing a bug on a website) and you want the AI to act like a smart worker doing a job.

> **Example:**
>
> - **Objective:** Fix the broken login button on my WordPress website.
> - **Success Criteria:** The button turns green when clicked and logs the user in.
> - **Scope:** Only change the login page. Do NOT change the database.
> - **Environment:** PHP 8.2, WordPress, Aussie time zone.

### 4. Gem Builder (Make a Custom AI)

Sometimes you want an AI to remember its rules forever. This tab helps you write "System Instructions" to build a permanent AI buddy (like a custom Gemini Gem).

> **Example:**
>
> - **Role:** You are an expert Math Tutor.
> - **Goal:** Help me study for my algebra test.
> - **Rules:** NEVER give me the final answer immediately. Always make me solve the first step myself. Use clear, encouraging language.

### 5. Code Companion (For Programmers)

When you are coding, you don't want the AI to be chatty. You just want the code! This tab forces the AI to be a strict, silent programming machine.

> **Example:**
>
> - **Framework:** PHP
> - **Persona:** Expert Backend Developer
> - **Operation:** Write a function to check if a user is logged in.
> - **Style:** Use Aussie date formats (dd/mm/yyyy).
> - _Strict Mode is ON, so the AI will ONLY output runnable code with zero talking!_

### 6. NotebookLM (The Podcast Director)

NotebookLM is a cool tool that turns your documents into AI podcasts. This tab tells the AI podcast hosts exactly what to talk about.

> **Example:**
>
> - **Source Focus:** Only look at my uploaded Science History notes.
> - **Audio Director:** Host 1 should be super excited about space. Host 2 should act like a skeptical scientist asking hard questions. Focus the debate on the moon landing!

### 7. Few-Shot (The Data Cleaner)

AI learns best by example. If you have a messy list and want the AI to organize it, you give it 2 or 3 examples of how to do it right.

> **Example:**
>
> - **System:** Turn messy notes into clean data.
> - **Example 1 Input:** "Met John today in Sydney."
> - **Example 1 Output:** `Name: John, City: Sydney`
> - **Example 2 Input:** "Talked to Sarah from Brisbane."
> - **Example 2 Output:** `Name: Sarah, City: Brisbane`

### 8. Deep Research (The Web Explorer)

When you want the AI to search the internet for you, you have to tell it where it is allowed to look, so it doesn't bring back fake news!

> **Example:**
>
> - **Hypothesis:** Find out if there are new rules for skateparks in Queensland in 2026.
> - **Trusted Sources:** Only use `.gov.au` websites.
> - **Excluded Sources:** Do not use Reddit, Facebook, or opinion blogs.
> - **Contradictions:** If two websites disagree, make a table showing both sides.

## 🌟 Super Cool Features Built-In

- **💾 Autosave:** If you accidentally close the tab, don't panic! Just click **"Restore Draft"** and your work comes right back.
- **📂 Save to Database:** Click "Generate & Save" to store your favorite prompts forever. You can search for them later using the search bar at the bottom!
- **👀 Live Preview:** Watch your prompt build itself in real-time on the right side of the screen as you type.
- **📋 One-Click Copy:** Click the "Copy" button to instantly copy your perfectly formatted prompt to your clipboard.

## 🧠 3 Pro-Tips for Middle Schoolers

1. **Be Bossy (But Polite):** AI is a computer, not a person. It likes strict rules! Tell it exactly what to do.
2. **Give it an Identity:** AI is way smarter when it's roleplaying. Always start with "You are an expert at..."
3. **Use Constraints:** Tell the AI what it is _NOT_ allowed to do (e.g., "Do not use big words," or "Do not write more than 3 paragraphs").
