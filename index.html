Workspace PTCF Method

Using Google Workspace's recommended methodology:

  • Persona: Who is the AI acting as?
  • Task: What exactly do you need it to do? (Start with an action verb)
  • Context: What background information is needed?
  • Format: How should the output be structured?

DESIGN Method (Image Generation)

Using the DESIGN Method:

  • Description
  • Environment
  • Style
  • Illumination
  • Gradation
  • Nuances
